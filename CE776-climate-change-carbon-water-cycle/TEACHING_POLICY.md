# CE776 teaching and materials policy

CE776 is taught in English. Slides, notebooks, workbook labels, exercise questions and course pages use concise, natural English.

- Research questions, carbon–water connections, observation processing, model and statistical assumptions, reproducibility, and interpretation are the course priorities.
- Provide practice only when it serves a specific learning question. AI assistance is optional.
- State exercise questions as complete sentences. Keep worked solutions concise, with equations, units and interpretation.
- Preserve the original slide design, alignment, fonts, figures and example flow. Use clear diagrams and calculations; avoid dense text.
- Keep teacher-only provenance and old slide correspondences separate from student materials.
- Keep common teaching materials independent of the offering year. Record actual observation and publication dates accurately.
- Manage class dates, times, deadlines and assessment arrangements in [course information](CURRENT_OFFERING.md).
- Replace holidays or unavoidable schedule conflicts with an online video lecture for the affected topic.
- Prepare and review PPTX files first. Convert to PDF only when separately requested by the instructor.
